Cristiano Ronaldo is widely regarded as one of the greatest players to ever perform a bicycle kick, with his 2018 UEFA Champions League goal against Juventus often cited as the pinnacle of the move in modern football.  The goal, scored at 2.38 meters in height, was so extraordinary that it earned a standing ovation from the Juventus crowd—a rare tribute to an opposing player. It was named one of the greatest goals in Champions League history and remains a defining moment in Ronaldo’s career.
